# Product Marketing Skill

## Overview

Product marketing aligns product capabilities with audience needs through strategic positioning and messaging.

## Frameworks

### Jobs-to-be-Done

- What job is the customer hiring this product to do?
- What are the circumstances of the job?
- What are the current alternatives?

### Audience Segmentation

- Personas: Who are they? What do they care about?
- ICPs (Ideal Customer Profiles): Firmographics, technographics
- Use cases: How will they use this?

### Positioning

- Category: What space do we play in?
- Differentiation: Why us vs competitors?
- Value prop: The promise we make

### Messaging

- Key messages: 3-5 core points
- Proof points: Evidence that supports messages
- Narrative: The story that connects everything

### Go-to-Market

- Channels: Where do they hang out?
- Pricing: How much will they pay?
- Launch: When and how do we introduce this?
