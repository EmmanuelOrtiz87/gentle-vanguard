# Customer Research Skill

## Overview

Systematic research to understand customers and validate assumptions.

## Research Methods

### Jobs-to-be-Done Interviews

- Uncover the real job customers are doing
- What progress are they trying to make?
- What are the struggling moments?

### Switch Interviews

- Why did they switch from the old solution?
- What triggered the switch?
- What almost stopped them?

### Surveys & Quantitative Research

- Validate hypotheses at scale
- Segment responses by persona
- Statistical significance

### User Testing Sessions

- Observe real usage
- Identify friction points
- Validate solutions

## Deliverables

- Persona canvas
- ICP definition
- Jobs map
- Insights report
