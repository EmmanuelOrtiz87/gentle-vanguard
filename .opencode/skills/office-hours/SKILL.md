# Office Hours Skill

## Overview
Structured product interrogation using 6 forcing questions.

## The 6 Forcing Questions

1. **What is the hardest part?**
   - Identify the biggest bottleneck
   - What slows users down?

2. **What do users really want?**
   - Not what they say, but what they do
   - Jobs they hire the product to do

3. **Why now?**
   - Market timing
   - Urgency factors
   - Window of opportunity

4. **Why you?**
   - Differentiation
   - Competitive moat
   - Unfair advantage

5. **What if it fails?**
   - Risk assessment
   - Mitigation strategies
   - Plan B

6. **What does success look like?**
   - Metrics
   - Outcomes
   - North star

## Process
1. Pose all 6 questions
2. Document answers
3. Reframe based on insights
4. Create strategic decisions doc

## Output
Strategic decisions document with rationale
